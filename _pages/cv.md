Research Experience
======
* **Ludwig Maximilian University of Munich**, Munich, Germany, June 2023 – Present
  * Advisor: Professor Wiktor Młynarski
  * Investigating joint sensory-behavioral representations for state estimation by extending efficient coding frameworks to account for the modulation of visual inputs by head and eye movements.
  * Developed a normative theory based on efficient coding and natural scene statistics to explain modulations of neural coding in the visual systems of diverse species during locomotion.

* **Technical University of Munich**, Munich, Germany, October 2022 – April 2023
  * Advisor: Professor Julijana Gjorgjieva
  * Characterized the effect of inhibitory to excitatory synaptic plasticity mechanisms on the balance of excitation and inhibition in dendritic spiking neural networks.
  * Investigated the formation of Hebbian assemblies with different combinations of plasticity mechanisms.

* **MIT Center for Brains, Minds, and Machines**, Cambridge, MA, USA, March 2021 – December 2021
  * Advisors: Dr. Arturo Deza and Professor Tomaso Poggio
  * Implemented a fully differentiable foveated texture transform module for a Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) in PyTorch and showed that the module increased the adversarial robustness of the network for scene classification.
  * Investigated the effect stochasticity in biologically inspired mechanisms has on the adversarial robustness and learned representations of CNNs.

* **University of Florida McKnight Brain Institute**, Gainesville, FL, USA, December 2019 – April 2022
  * Advisor: Professor Thomas Mareci
  * Investigated the efficacy of novel edge weight definitions for the analysis of brain structural networks.
  * Created the Diffusion Image Network Simulator (DINS), a Python program that simulates diffusion MRI images with complex white matter fiber connectivity patterns.
  * Classified and characterized structural brain networks in Alzheimer's disease patients using topological data analysis and machine learning.
