I am a fast-track Ph.D. candidate in Neuroscience at the [Graduate School of Systemic Neurosciences](https://www.gsn.uni-muenchen.de/) at LMU Munich, working with Professor Wiktor Młynarski. I study how and why behavior modulates sensory processing, through the lens of natural scene statistics, efficient coding, information theory and population coding.

Outside of research, I enjoy science outreach and community building. I serve on the organizing committee of Talking Science in Munich and have coordinated NeuroCamp, a neuroscience summer school for high school students.
