I am a doctoral researcher in theoretical neuroscience at the [Graduate School of Systemic Neurosciences (GSN)](https://www.gsn.uni-muenchen.de/) at LMU Munich, working with Prof. Dr. Wiktor Młynarski. I study how and why behavior modulates sensory processing, through the lens of natural scene statistics, efficient coding, information theory and population coding.

Beyond research, I am involved in science outreach and community building. I am on the organizing committee of Talking Science (an annual BCCN Munich event), helped organize a student led conference at the GSN, and helped coordinate NeuroCamp (a neuroscience summer school for high school students).
